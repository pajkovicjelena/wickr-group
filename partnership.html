01  /  Decarbonization

Dereroy & Co.

Dereroy entered the decarbonization industry by providing two of its main sectors — Carbon Capture and Usage (CCU) and Power-to-Gas (PTG) energy storage — with the benefits of two revolutionary technologies for:

  • Direct recycling of captured carbon dioxide (CO₂) into synthetic hydrocarbon fuels
  • Direct synthesis of methane (CH₄) from atmospheric air

Both processes and the apparatuses to run them are developed and optimized for industrial use, based on an invention for universal, exothermic conversion of gaseous and liquid chemical elements and compounds. The ownership rights were acquired by Dereroy & Co., a.s. after an Invention Ownership Transfer Agreement with the inventor, in June 2019.

02  /  Rail engineering

Škoda Transportation

Škoda Transportation a.s. is a Czech engineering company that continues the legacy of Škoda Works' rolling-stock manufacturing, which began at the end of the 19th century in Plzeň. Today it develops and manufactures electric rail vehicles for urban and railway transport.

The company fully or partially owns numerous companies developing and manufacturing rolling stock, electric motors and related products across several European countries. The group is among the world's largest manufacturers of trams and propulsion units for trolleybuses, and a significant regional producer of railway vehicles.

03  /  Snowmaking systems

Supersnow SA

Supersnow designs and produces comprehensive snowmaking systems. For over 20 years its facilities have been found in leading ski resorts across most European countries, as well as in Asia, South America and North America — devices in over 30 countries worldwide.

Continuous development and consistent execution of its goals allowed Supersnow to open a branch in Roppen in 2017, in the Austrian Alps — home to a modern distribution centre, warehouse, logistics hub and the Supersnow service base.

04  /  Sports surfaces

Tarkett Sport

Tarkett Sports is part of the Tarkett Group. With a 140-year history, Tarkett is a worldwide leader in innovative flooring and sports-surface solutions, with net sales of €2.6 billion in 2020.

Offering vinyl, linoleum, rubber, carpet, wood, laminate, artificial turf and athletics tracks, the group serves customers in over 100 countries. With more than 12,000 employees and 33 industrial sites, Tarkett sells 1.3 million square meters of flooring every day — for hospitals, schools, housing, hotels, offices, stores and sports fields.

05  /  Natural turf

HELLASOD

HELLASOD is the largest sod producer in Greece, as well as the leading construction company in the sports-fields sector. A fully equipped sod-producing company, it also contracts sports and landscaping projects.

Through its ultra-modern nurseries and machinery, specialized staff and a large network of representatives all over Greece, HELLASOD is reliable — offering customers quality and credibility.
